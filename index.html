Morning · 08:10

The forest wakes first.

Northwind sits at the end of an old logging trail, ninety minutes past the last paved road. Mornings start under spruce and fir, with light falling through in slow gold columns.

There is no lobby and no schedule. Coffee is on the stove by seven, and the trail is yours.
